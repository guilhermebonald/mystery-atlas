About the channel

Mystery Atlas is a YouTube channel of short videos about real historical mysteries, lost cities, strange artifacts and forgotten events from every corner of the world.

Every fact comes from documented sources (Wikipedia and the historians and scientists it cites), and every image is credited and used under a free license or in the public domain.

How the videos are made

Videos are produced and uploaded by a private automation run by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube API Services only to upload videos to this channel and to read this channel's own analytics. See the Privacy Policy.
